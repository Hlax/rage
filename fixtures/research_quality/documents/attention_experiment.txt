Synthetic attention experiment

Background
Earlier laboratory studies linked noise exposure with fatigue (Nguyen, 2018).

Methods
Forty-eight adult volunteers completed four ten-minute attention blocks under quiet or intermittent-tone conditions.

Results
Participants exposed to intermittent tones made 12 percent more omission errors than participants in the quiet condition during the final task block.
Response-time differences were not statistically distinguishable in this sample.

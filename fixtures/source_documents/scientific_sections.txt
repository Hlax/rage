Synthetic Cross-Domain Trial
Authors: A. Researcher and B. Analyst

Abstract
This synthetic study evaluated a scheduling intervention across several workplaces. The intervention group reported lower fatigue than the comparison group.

Introduction
Prior studies disagree about whether schedule changes improve sustained performance. This study tests the question in a bounded sample.

Methods
One hundred participants were assigned to intervention and comparison schedules. Outcomes were measured weekly for six weeks.

Results
The intervention group reported lower fatigue and completed more scheduled tasks. The comparison group showed no material change.

Discussion
The findings suggest that schedule design may affect fatigue in similar workplace settings. Causal interpretation remains limited to this design.

Limitations
The sample was synthetic, follow-up was short, and the study did not measure long-term retention.
